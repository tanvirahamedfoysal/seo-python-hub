Public browsing

Reading public pages does not require an account. The service may receive ordinary technical request information needed to deliver pages, protect the service, and understand aggregate usage.

Account information

If you use private learner features, the app may store your account identity, progress, bookmarks, and preferences so those features work across sessions. We do not make private progress visible on public pages.

Your choices

You should be able to review, correct, export, or delete personal information through the account experience or by contacting the team.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the contact page. This page is a product draft, not legal advice.
